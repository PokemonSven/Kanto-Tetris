IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
The game references the artists' hosted image files at runtime rather than
silently repackaging unrelated DeviantArt works. An internet connection is
required for the fan-art portraits and badge art to load.

Pokémon and the underlying Pokémon / Kanto badge designs belong to their
respective rights holders. This project is unofficial and non-commercial.
