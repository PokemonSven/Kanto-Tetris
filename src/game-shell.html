PROF. OAK — TRAINER GUIDE CALL

“Good timing! Let’s check your route plan, battle rules, and training options before you head out.”

HOW BATTLES WORK

  • Clear lines to attack. Your Pokémon attacks first; if the opponent survives, it counterattacks.
  • Doubles, Triples, and Tetrises hit much harder than Singles, so strong Tetris play means fewer enemy counters.
  • Wild fights are forgiving training battles. Gym Leaders are the real team-building tests.

TYPES MATTER

  • Pokémon level, stats, and type matchup affect damage.
  • Your Pokémon automatically uses whichever of its own types gives the better matchup.
  • Super-effective Pokémon have a real advantage, but matchups are softened enough that excellent Tetris can still overcome a bad fight.

WHEN YOU GET A TETRIS

  • A four-line TETRIS gives your strongest battle attack.
  • Your whole party below the current level cap receives Team Share XP.
  • During a wild encounter, each Tetris also adds +10% to that Pokémon's catch chance for the current fight.

ITEMS

  • You may carry 3 items. Extra items can be stored in Sven's PC.
  • Passive items only work while carried; items stored in the PC give no passive bonus.
  • Items can heal/revive Pokémon, improve catches, raise levels, clear the board, alter speed, or provide other special advantages.

REWARDS + XP

  • The active Pokémon earns Battle XP for defeating wild and Gym Pokémon. Tetrises give Team Share XP.
  • Side quests award money and items; 3 of the 30 quests award a mystery non-legendary Pokémon.
  • Your current level cap is 2 levels above the next Gym Leader's highest-level Pokémon.

GYMS + TRAINING

  • Complete the route goal to reach the next city. At the city you may heal, train, or challenge the Gym Leader.
  • Training repeats the previous route indefinitely while Gym progress stays frozen. Training lines never count toward the following city.
  • Only defeating the Gym Leader unlocks the next route, resets route progress to 0, and allows progress toward the next city to begin.

ROGUE VS. ADVENTURE

  • ROGUE: Topping out the Tetris board ends the run.
  • ADVENTURE: Your campaign survives Gym losses. Losing to a Gym Leader returns you to that city with heal, shop, train, and rematch options.
  • Adventure Easy stays at level 1 unless you raise the speed/score slider. Only Gym Badges raise speed: Normal adds one level per badge and caps at 8; Hard adds two and caps at 12. Line clears and entering battles do not raise speed. Quest rewards are unchanged.
PROF. OAK TIP: Wild routes build your team. Train as long as you want, then challenge the Gym when you feel ready. Progress toward the next city begins only after you win the badge.
