TETRIS CATCH: KANTO 150 — CLASSICAL CHIPTUNE UPDATE
====================================================

MUSIC
This build replaces the old short procedural loops with ten longer Game Boy-style chiptune arrangements generated by the game's upgraded music engine based on public-domain classical compositions.
No official Pokémon music and no third-party orchestra recordings are bundled.

Soundtrack:
- TITLE — Mozart: Eine kleine Nachtmusik, I. Allegro
- BROCK — Grieg: In the Hall of the Mountain King
- MISTY — Saint-Saëns: Aquarium (The Carnival of the Animals)
- LT. SURGE — Vivaldi: Summer, III. Presto (The Four Seasons)
- ERIKA — Tchaikovsky: Waltz of the Flowers (The Nutcracker)
- KOGA — J.S. Bach: Toccata and Fugue in D Minor, BWV 565
- SABRINA — Beethoven: Moonlight Sonata, III. Presto agitato
- BLAINE — Vivaldi: Winter, I. Allegro non molto (The Four Seasons)
- GIOVANNI — Mozart: Requiem, Dies Irae
- VICTORY — Beethoven: Ode to Joy

ARRANGEMENT / PLAYBACK
- The arrangements are encoded specifically for this game and synthesized live with the Web Audio API.
- Pulse-wave lead, pulse harmony/arpeggios, triangle-style bass and noise percussion
  are used to evoke an early handheld / Game Boy sound palette.
- The music bus uses gentler pulse levels and a low-pass filter so the soundtrack is less harsh during long sessions.
- Most tracks run roughly 45–55 seconds before looping instead of repeating a tiny motif.
- The title music begins after the player's first interaction, satisfying browser autoplay rules.
- Choosing a starter / continuing a run switches to the current Gym's soundtrack.
- Each Gym maps to its own classical theme.
- Earning the eighth badge switches to Ode to Joy.
- MUSIC, SFX and manual TRACK controls remain available at the top of the UI.

PUBLIC-DOMAIN COMPOSITION REFERENCES
The underlying compositions are public-domain works. The project uses its own note arrangements and live synthesis rather than copying modern commercial recordings. Reference scores
and public-domain editions include Mutopia Project and IMSLP entries for the works above.
See CREDITS.txt for details.

GAMEPLAY
All existing route encounters, roguelike persistence, Gym battles, team systems,
calibrated sprites, badges, manual crop editor and save behavior are retained.

GYM LEADER BOSS BATTLES
-----------------------
- Reaching a Gym line goal starts a Gym Leader battle instead of awarding the badge immediately.
- Gym Leader teams and levels follow Pokemon FireRed/LeafGreen rosters.
- Defeat the full Leader team to earn the badge and raise drop speed.
- Active Pokemon levels are capped at the ace level of the upcoming Gym.
- Pokemon caught during the run enter close to the current level cap so team switching stays viable.
- Charged partner moves fire automatically during a line-clear battle action.
- Bill's PC is unavailable while a Gym Leader battle is active.
- If the whole team faints during a Gym battle, the usual 15% score penalty applies,
  the team heals, and five more lines are required for a rematch.

GYM LEADER ART
--------------
Gym Leader portraits use a credited online fan-art set by PhilDragash, linked in CREDITS.txt.
Internet access is required for that portrait sheet to load.

MANUAL CROP EDITOR
------------------
Open CROP_EDITOR.html directly or click CROP EDITOR in the game top bar.
- POKEMON SHEET shows the full uncropped 1792x448 sprite sheet.
- GYM LEADERS shows the full uncropped leader portrait sheet.
- Drag a box around the selected sprite/leader and click SAVE CROP.
- Saved crops apply immediately in that browser using localStorage.
- Use EXPORT DATA when you want to bake coordinates into a later build.
