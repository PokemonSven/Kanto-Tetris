PROF. OAK'S TRAINER GUIDE

“There’s a time and place for everything, but not now! Actually… yes now. Let’s review your journey!”

HOW TO PLAY

  • Clear Tetris lines to attack the current wild Pokémon.
  • Reach the Gym line goal to enter town, heal/shop, then challenge the Gym Leader.
  • Beat every Pokémon on the Leader’s team to earn the badge.

WILD POKéMON + ROUTES

  • Encounters depend on your current Kanto route.
  • As you earn badges, the route pool changes.
  • Some Pokémon are common, uncommon, rare, very rare, or legendary.

CATCHING POKéMON

  • You only get one catch attempt after defeating a wild Pokémon.
  • Each Tetris during that encounter boosts catch odds by 10%.
  • Your first six Pokémon join the team. Extras go to Bill’s PC.

EXP + LEVELS

  • Your active partner gains Battle XP from clears and victories.
  • Level caps are tied to your current Gym badge progress.
  • Pokémon can evolve by level. Everstone blocks evolution while held.

DAMAGE

  • 1-line, 2-line, 3-line, and 4-line clears deal increasing base damage.
  • Gym battles reduce base damage slightly, so Leaders are tougher.
  • If the enemy survives your clear, it counterattacks your active partner.

ENERGY MATCHING

  • Matching same-colored Energy blocks inside cleared rows gives score bonuses.
  • Bigger matching runs give bigger bonuses.
  • Energy chains can add bonus damage and may be super effective or not very effective.

MOVE CHARGING + AUTO FIRE

  • Your active Pokémon has a charged move based on its type.
  • Clearing matching Energy charges that move.
  • When fully charged, the move auto-fires during battle.

ITEMS

  • You can carry up to 3 items in the Item Bar.
  • Click usable items to activate them.
  • Extra reward items go to Sven’s PC. Use Sven’s PC to withdraw or store items.

REWARDS

  • Wild catches give a small money reward.
  • Gym wins give stronger rewards and can unlock better item pools.
  • Side quests give money, items, and sometimes rare Pokémon.

BADGES + SPEED

  • Each badge increases drop speed by 5%.
  • Later badges unlock stronger rewards.
  • The run gets faster and harder as you travel through Kanto.

GYM LEADER SPECIALS

  • Brock: Stone Drop • Misty: Rising Tide • Lt. Surge: Board Shock • Erika: Overgrowth
  • Koga: Poison Spikes • Sabrina: Psychic Blind • Blaine: Flame Column • Giovanni: Earthquake
PROF. OAK TIP: Build a balanced team, switch active partners to train under the level cap, and use your items before a Gym fight gets out of hand.
